Is My Data Safe During Electronics Recycling?

NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 sets the federal standard for media sanitization. The guidelines require that sanitization render data recovery "infeasible using state of the art laboratory techniques," the requirement STS destruction meets for Billings organizations. We execute destruction that exceeds these protocols for every data-bearing device.

Institutions such as St. Vincent Healthcare require vendors capable of executing documented, verifiable destruction. By providing NAID AAA certified data erasure processes, STS protects clients from costly privacy incidents.
