Suggestions to make it better, from our vantage:
1. Pull schema generation OUT of the model. We emit JSON-LD from a deterministic, version-locked library (a connected @graph), not a prompt. It removes hallucinated @id / duplicate-singleton / conflicting-node risk entirely. This is the single biggest reliability win available to you, and your checkers become a backstop rather than the primary defense.
2. Make the headless-delivery contract permanent and explicit. You already found the chat-UI prompt failure (models narrating instead of emitting HTML); bake that neutralizer in as a first-class layer so it can never regress.
3. If you ever want this to serve more than STS, fact-layer the hardcodes (business name, baseDomain, sacred links, registry) so the masters take a client fact object. We can share our fact-layer + drift/bump pattern.
4. Consider distributable enforcement: we ship the gate as installed teeth (pre-commit hook + gate.config + a sync manifest) into each client repo so output cannot drift from doctrine. Useful if the engine ever leaves your hands.
