Confirmed CID parse (this is the tricky bit):

Maps URL data segment: !1s0x5495a0b27175b345:0x8ac16143d1506315
Take the hex after the colon: 0x8ac16143d1506315
Convert to decimal: 9998379591641621269
Final map URL: https://www.google.com/maps?cid=9998379591641621269
Geo coordinates come from the !3d<lat>!4d<lng> pin in the URL. The parser must also extract street/city/state/zip, phone, 7-day hours (including 24-hour days), and plus code — and fail loud on any missing/unparseable required field rather than inventing it.

What the enriched GMB schema must contain
A publish-bound GMB page's JSON-LD must carry the full local signal set:

copy/paste-ready into a Joomla <head> as <script type="application/ld+json">…</script>
