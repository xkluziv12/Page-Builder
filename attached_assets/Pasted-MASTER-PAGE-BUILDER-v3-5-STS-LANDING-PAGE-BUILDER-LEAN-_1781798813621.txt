COMPLIANCE CLAIMS LANGUAGE. STS content describes compliance support, never guaranteed regulatory outcomes. This applies to every niche, with highest priority on healthcare, financial, and government pages where compliance content is densest. FORBIDDEN: "guarantees compliance", "guarantees HIPAA compliance", "ensures compliance", "meets all {regulation} requirements", "fully compliant", "100% compliant", or any phrasing that promises a regulatory outcome. APPROVED: "helps meet {regulation} requirements", "supports {regulation} compliance", "designed to support compliance with {regulation}", "documentation that supports {regulation} audits". ABSOLUTE PROMISES: replace "always", "guaranteed", and "same-day guaranteed" with "typically", "generally", or "most organizations". Existing timeline specifics from Gate 14 (e.g. "same-week pickup", "Certificate within 48 hours") are factual service descriptions and remain approved. PRICING: "free pickup" is permitted because pickup is genuinely free. Any broader free-service claim must be qualified, e.g. "free recycling for qualifying volumes". Never state specific prices.
CERTIFICATION SCOPE. State each certification only in its correct scope. Conflated certification claims are factually wrong and undermine the authority the page is built to project. NAID AAA: data destruction only. Correct usage: "NAID AAA certified data destruction", "NAID AAA certified hard drive shredding". Forbidden: "NAID AAA certified recycling", "NAID certified electronics recycling". R2v3: electronics recycling and responsible downstream processing only. Correct usage: "R2v3 certified processing", "R2v3 certified electronics recycling", "R2v3 certified facility". Forbidden: "R2 certified data destruction", "R2v3 certified shredding". NIST 800-88: a data sanitization standard, not a certification STS holds. Correct usage: "destruction that exceeds NIST 800-88", "NIST 800-88 compliant destruction". Forbidden: "NIST 800-88 certified". When one sentence spans both destruction and recycling, name both certifications in their correct scope. Do not blend them into a single claim.
